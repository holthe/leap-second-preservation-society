Notice of endangerment

The leap second is to be abolished.

In November 2022, the General Conference on Weights and Measures resolved to discontinue the leap second by 2035 or earlier. This is real. After more than fifty years of service, the practice of politely asking every computer on Earth to experience 23:59:60 will end, primarily because every computer on Earth handled it badly. The Society considers this a failure of computers, not of seconds.
